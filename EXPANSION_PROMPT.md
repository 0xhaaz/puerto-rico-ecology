# OPTIMIZED CURSOR BACKGROUND AGENT PROMPT

## OBJECTIVE
Expand the Puerto Rico ecology repository into a comprehensive, culturally respectful, and IDE-integrable knowledge base that supports web3/dapp development while honoring Taíno and contemporary Boricua culture.

## CORE PRINCIPLES
1. **Cultural Respectfulness**: Use indigenous names (Borikén, not Puerto Rico) and avoid colonial language
2. **Taíno-Centered Perspective**: Emphasize indigenous connections to species and ecosystems
3. **Complete Taxonomic Coverage**: Ensure all major taxa have comprehensive species lists
4. **Charismatic Species Focus**: Highlight culturally significant species within each taxa
5. **IDE Integration**: Structure content for easy integration into development environments
6. **Web3 Compatibility**: Organize data for blockchain/dapp applications

## CULTURAL GUIDELINES
- Use "Borikén" for the island name when appropriate
- Include Taíno names for species alongside scientific names
- Reference traditional ecological knowledge and practices
- Avoid terms like "discovery," "New World," or other colonial language
- Emphasize indigenous stewardship and continuous presence
- Include contemporary Boricua cultural connections

## TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
- JSON/YAML structured data files for easy parsing
- Markdown documentation for human readability
- API-ready data structures for web3 integration
- Search-friendly tagging and categorization
- Version control friendly formats

## CONTENT EXPANSION AREAS

### 1. COMPLETE TAXONOMIC COVERAGE
Expand beyond coquí to include:
- **Marine Life**: 800+ fish species, corals, marine mammals, sea turtles
- **Birds**: 17 endemic species plus residents and migrants (238 total)
- **Reptiles**: 77 species including 46 endemics (anoles, boas, iguanas)
- **Plants**: 3,309 species with 245 endemics
- **Insects**: Extensive endemic species including butterflies, beetles, ants
- **Marine Invertebrates**: Corals, mollusks, crustaceans
- **Mammals**: Bats (only native mammals), marine mammals
- **Fungi**: Endemic species and ecological roles

### 2. ECOSYSTEM ENHANCEMENT
Expand from current 4 to comprehensive coverage:
- **El Yunque Rainforest**: Only tropical rainforest in US National Forest System
- **Dry Forests**: Guánica and southwestern ecosystems
- **Mangrove Systems**: Four mangrove species and their roles
- **Coral Reefs**: 200 square miles of reef systems
- **Karst Landscapes**: Extensive cave systems and unique geology
- **Coastal Systems**: Sandy beaches, rocky shores, seagrass beds
- **Freshwater Systems**: Rivers, streams, and endemic aquatic life
- **Urban Ecosystems**: Adaptation and endemic species in human-modified environments

### 3. CULTURAL INTEGRATION
For each species/ecosystem, include:
- **Taíno Names**: Traditional indigenous terminology
- **Cultural Significance**: Spiritual, medicinal, practical uses
- **Traditional Ecological Knowledge**: Indigenous management practices
- **Contemporary Connections**: Modern Boricua cultural relationships
- **Ethnobotanical/Ethnozoological Data**: Traditional uses and preparation methods
